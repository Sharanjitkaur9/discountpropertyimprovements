Decks utilise H5-treated piles, H3.2 joists, and 140mm kiln-dried Kwila hardwood with secret stainless-steel fastenings to resist warping under direct Canterbury UV exposure.
Yes, 100% compliant. All our wet area installations adhere strictly to New Zealand Building Code Clause E3/AS1 (Internal Moisture). We apply BRANZ-appraised waterproof elastomeric membranes with reinforcing tape across all hob, internal corner, and floor-to-wall junctions before laying tiles. In walk-in showers and splash zones, we ensure correct 1:60 to 1:80 falls to waste drains, flood test the pan, and supply standard producer documentation for council compliance and insurance peace of mind.
Yes, that is our core strength. Rather than hiring separate subbies for joinery, flooring, tiling, painting, and outdoor carpentry — which leads to scheduling delays and finger-pointing — our in-house multi-skilled team coordinates the entire sequence. We handle subfloor leveling first, run concealed electrical conduits, install wall panelling, lay timber/vinyl flooring with precision undercut jambs, and finish skirting trims seamlessly under a single point of contact and guaranteed timeline.
Our SPC (Stone Plastic Composite) planks feature a dense limestone-polymer core that is 100% waterproof and dimensionally stable through Canterbury seasonal temperature swings. Unlike basic laminate, SPC will not swell or cup from spills or high laundry humidity. Each board includes a commercial 0.55mm wear layer with scratch-resistant ceramic bead coating and integrated 1.5mm acoustic IXPE underlay that absorbs footstep noise and eliminates telegraphing from minor subfloor irregularities.
Our panels achieve an NRC (Noise Reduction Coefficient) rating of up to 0.85 when installed over standard furring battens. The 3D solid timber vertical slats disrupt specular flutter echoes, while the 9mm high-density recycled PET polyester backing absorbs mid-to-high frequency sound waves. In modern open-plan Canterbury homes with timber floors and large glass ranch sliders, this drastically cuts hollow acoustic bounce and makes TV audio and conversation crisp and calm.
Yes, 100% invisible. We build recessed in-wall conduit chases into the stud structure behind the timber panels, with brush pass-through plates located directly behind your TV bracket. Heavy-duty TV wall mounts (including flush or articulating brackets) are bolted into structural nogs. We can also integrate concealed wiring runs down into a cantilevered floating media console or side cabinet for consoles, subwoofers, and Apple TVs.
Because we pre-measure with millimeter-precise laser tools and pre-cut, mitre, and finish all panels offsite in our workshop, on-site installation for a feature wall typically takes only 1 to 2 business days. Timber flooring installations are usually wrapped up in 2 days, and typical boundary fencing takes 3 days. Clean-site protocols (dust extractors and heavy-duty floor runners) ensure zero disruption to your daily routine.

For illumination, we embed continuous aluminium channels with dot-free diffused 3000K warm white LED strips, dimmable via remote, smart home app, or wall switch.
